About Jumcent Properties

Your dependable partner for serviced houses and short-let apartments in the UK and beyond

Who We Are

Jumcent Properties specialises in comfortable, fully furnished short-let homes designed around the real-world needs of business travellers, contractor teams, relocating families, and leisure guests.

With established UK residences in Crewe, Stoke-on-Trent, and Newcastle upon Tyne—alongside our international serviced apartment in Akure—we combine attentive property management with practical home comforts.

Every home in our portfolio is selected for strong transport links, reliable parking, fast Wi-Fi, and spacious self-catering facilities so guests can settle in effortlessly from day one.
