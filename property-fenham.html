Fenham Place

📍 Newcastle upon Tyne, United Kingdom

About This Property

Relax with family or colleagues at Fenham Place—a peaceful, well-connected 3-bedroom home in Newcastle upon Tyne offering an inviting blend of comfort, space, and city access.

Featuring three generously sized bedrooms, a sleek modern kitchen with full cooking and laundry facilities, a tiled bath-and-shower bathroom, and a cosy lounge, the property is equipped for effortless short breaks or multi-week stays.

Positioned within easy reach of Newcastle city centre, Royal Victoria Infirmary (RVI), Newcastle University, St. James' Park, local shops, and A1/A167 road links across the North East.
