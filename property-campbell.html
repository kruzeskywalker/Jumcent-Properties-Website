Campbell Place

📍 Stoke-on-Trent, Staffordshire, United Kingdom

About This Property

Ideal for contractor crews, families, tourists, and working professionals, Campbell Place is a spacious 4-bedroom home in Stoke-on-Trent sleeping up to 6 guests across 5 separate beds (with an optional sofa bed in the living room).

Bright and comfortable throughout, the house is well-suited for both short breaks and multi-week project stays. It offers a full self-catering kitchen with double oven, comfortable dining and lounge areas, laundry appliances, and a private enclosed garden.

Conveniently situated near Royal Stoke University Hospital, Hanley town centre, Festival Park, Waterworld, and Alton Towers Resort (~30 minutes by car), with fast links onto the A500, A50, and M6.
