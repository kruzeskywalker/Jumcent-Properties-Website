SkyHouse Shortlet Apartment

📍 Owode Area, Oba-Ile, Akure, Ondo State, Nigeria

About This Property

Welcome to SkyHouse, a modern and serene 1-bedroom serviced apartment in the peaceful Owode Area of Oba-Ile, Akure. Designed for comfort and peace of mind, this residence is ideal for visiting professionals, diaspora travellers, and couples seeking a quiet, well-managed base.

Enjoy uninterrupted comfort with 24-hour power supply (backed by inverter power) and fast, reliable internet (Starlink/fibre), making remote work effortless. The apartment includes an air-conditioned bedroom, a clean modern bathroom, and an in-unit washer and dryer.

Guests also benefit from generous gated parking on-site and quick road access to both Akure Airport and Akure city centre.
