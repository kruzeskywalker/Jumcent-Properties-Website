Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a property?

Each property page includes direct links to book immediately on Airbnb or Booking.com. For extended stays, corporate bookings, or bespoke quotes, contact us directly via our form or WhatsApp.

What is the minimum stay?

Minimum stay requirements vary by property and season, typically starting at 2–3 nights, with discounted weekly and monthly options available.

Is professional cleaning included?

Yes, every stay includes professional pre-arrival cleaning and fresh bed linen and towels. Mid-stay housekeeping can also be arranged for extended bookings.

Do you offer contractor and corporate rates?

Yes—we welcome contractor teams, business travellers, and relocation stays, offering preferential rates and invoicing on stays of 7+ nights.
