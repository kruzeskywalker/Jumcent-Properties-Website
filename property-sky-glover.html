About This Property

Welcome to Sky-Glover House, a welcoming 2-bedroom terraced home on Glover Terrace in Crewe, Cheshire East. Thoughtfully prepared for business travellers, contractor teams, and families, it delivers the privacy and practicality of a full home.

Inside you'll find two comfortable bedrooms with room-darkening blinds, a bright sitting room with a Smart TV, a dedicated workspace, and a fully equipped kitchen complete with oven, hob, microwave, dishwasher, and washing machine.

Conveniently positioned in Crewe with quick links to Crewe Railway Station, Leighton Hospital, Bentley Motors, local supermarkets, and Junction 16/17 of the M6 motorway for travel across Cheshire and Staffordshire.
