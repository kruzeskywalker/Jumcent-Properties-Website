About This Property

Welcome to Sky-Glover House, a charming terraced home on Glover Terrace in Crewe, Cheshire East. Managed by Sigma Stays, this beautifully presented property offers the perfect blend of comfort and convenience for your stay.

The property features well-appointed bedrooms, a fully equipped kitchen, and a warm and inviting living area — everything you need for a comfortable home away from home. Whether you're visiting for business or leisure, this property is designed to make your stay effortless.

Ideally located in Crewe with excellent transport links, you'll have easy access to Crewe railway station, local shops, restaurants, and the picturesque Cheshire countryside. The M6 motorway is also within easy reach for wider travel across the North West.
