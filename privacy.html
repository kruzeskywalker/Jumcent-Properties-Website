Privacy & Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Jumcent Properties ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the Data Protection Act 2018, and the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR).

1. Who We Are & Contact Details

For enquiries submitted through jumcentproperties.co.uk, Jumcent Properties acts as the data controller. You can contact us regarding data protection matters at:

  • Email: info@skykruze.com
  • Telephone: +44 7751 573 029
  • Location: London, United Kingdom

2. Personal Data We Collect

We only collect personal data that you voluntarily provide or consent to share:

  • Enquiry & Direct Booking Data: Full name, email address, optional phone/WhatsApp number, property of interest, and message details submitted through our Contact Form, email, phone, or WhatsApp.
  • Optional Usage Analytics: Anonymised page-view and interaction metrics via Google Analytics 4, collected only if you click "Accept Analytics" on our cookie banner.

3. How & Why We Use Your Data (Lawful Basis)

  • Responding to Enquiries & Pre-Contractual Steps (Article 6(1)(b) & 6(1)(f) UK GDPR): To answer availability questions, prepare corporate quotes, and manage direct short-let bookings.
  • Website Analytics (Article 6(1)(a) Consent): To understand which property listings are most helpful to visitors when you opt in to analytics cookies.
  • Legal & Accounting Obligations (Article 6(1)(c)): Maintaining booking and invoice records where required by tax and hospitality regulations.

4. Third-Party Processors & Booking Platforms

  • Formspree Inc.: Processes contact form submissions securely on our behalf in order to deliver your message to our inbox.
  • Airbnb & Booking.com: When you click external booking buttons to reserve a stay on Airbnb or Booking.com, your reservation and payment are governed by that platform's own privacy notice and terms.
  • Google Ireland / Google LLC: Provides optional Google Analytics (`G-N89EW0TN2Y`) with IP anonymisation enabled, strictly after you grant consent.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

We keep our use of browser storage minimal and transparent:

  • Strictly Necessary Preference (`jumcent_cookie_consent_v1`): Stores whether you chose "Essential Only" or "Accept Analytics" so you are not prompted on every page load.
  • Optional Analytics Cookies (`_ga`, `_ga_*`): Set by Google Analytics only if you opt in. No analytics scripts or cookies load if you select "Essential Only" or ignore the banner.

6. Data Retention

General enquiries that do not result in a booking are retained for up to 12 months to support follow-up questions, then securely deleted. Records relating to completed direct bookings or corporate invoices are retained for up to 6 years in line with UK tax requirements.

7. Your UK GDPR Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, to restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw analytics consent at any time. To exercise any right, email info@skykruze.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
